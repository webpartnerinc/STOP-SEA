Our story

A hidden gem on South Main Street

Stop and Sea is a family-owned seafood restaurant in Bellefontaine, Ohio. We keep it simple: fresh fish, a hot fryer, fries cut by hand, and burgers straight off the grill — all served with genuine, small-town hospitality.

Gluten-free diners are family here. With celiac disease in our own family, we take it seriously — nearly everything can be made gluten-free, prepared with care so you can order with confidence.

  • Family-owned & operated
  • Extensive gluten-free options
  • An owner who checks on your table
